# Pini's feedback on the new Android design (26.11.2026, after using build 0.1.954)

Source: Pini's own words (translated), plus his wife's general feedback. Raw, unprioritised. Two work streams: A = UX/design (Fable designer), B = bugs (Sonnet implementer).

## General (his wife, a fresh user, old design vs new design)
The new app looks far more mature and professional, but it is very unclear what does what, where things live, what to do and how. This is a UX problem, not a look problem.

## A. UX and design (rule 7: canvas first, Pini approves before code)
1. **The village is not understood as the menu of everything else.** Not clear enough.
2. **The menu that opens on tapping the village** does not read as a menu: it looks like a plain list inside the sheet that rises. Needs a significant redesign.
3. **"Your trip" screen:** it is not clear that everything can be edited.
4. **Ticket cut (perforation) animation:** the card cut is not shown, only the sound plays.
5. **Ski prices card:** too much text; the design is too plain, unclear and not experiential. Especially the price shown on the screen itself (not in the sheet): does not look good.
6. **"Food and drink":** on tap it was not understood what opens, and it is certainly not food and drink or details of food places. It must open what it promises.
7. **"The group" (החבר'ה):** wording is bad and it is unclear what it does. Needs an experience and design change.
8. **Group list loading:** takes time, and the screen jumps when the groups arrive. Needs a loading experience that does not jump.
9. **"ערב בבקתה"** (the games): the name does not say these are games. Needs a design and experience change.
10. **Not on the mountain (location):** when the user is not on the mountain, the location marker on the live model goes wild and sticks to an unrelated place. Needs a proper user experience for "you are not at the mountain" when trying to find the location or start a recording.

## B. Bugs
B1. In the mountain picture (picture mode) the picture can be moved up and down; it feels bad. The picture must not move.
B2. Not at the mountain: the location marker on the live model jumps to an unrelated spot (the technical side of A10; the UX message comes from the designer).
B3. Live model: tapping a run makes the camera move away instead of closing in on the run.
