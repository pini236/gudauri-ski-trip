# The "מסלולים" button, version 4: five options

**When:** 27.11.2026, product designer (Fable). Design only, no code.
**Status:** waiting for Pini's pick (rule 7). Three questions in board `RN00`.
**Canvas:** https://claude.ai/artifact/8NrAKy97KWRj3JWcxx41yQ (version 101), new page "כפתור המסלולים, גרסה 4 (27.11)" (`runbtn4`): `RN00` (summary), `RN01` (all five up close, day and night, three states), and per option `RNk1` (day), `RNk2` (night, recording), `RNk3` (the opening, looping at real speed), k = 1 to 5. Source: `design/redesign/tools/rn_boards.py`. Pictures: `ref/`.
**Replaces:** `SW1`-`SW6` (version 3 button). **Unchanged:** the shelf of version 2 (`SV3`-`SV6`: chips, sorting, strip, cards), the word "מסלולים", the count. No new strings.

## What was cheap in the picture-in-button (version 3), and the fix

| Cheap | Fix (all five) |
|---|---|
| A drawn glyph (white triangle, three coloured squiggles) in front of the real mountain | Nothing is drawn: the app's own mountain picture, the real contour lines, or type only |
| A frame in a frame in a frame (circle in a pill in the bar), each with an outline | One idea per button, no outlines; depth comes from light and shadow |
| Four things in one button (glyph, word, count, a grey disc with a chevron that read as a second button) | The chevron is a bare chevron (E: one accent disc, on purpose) |
| The most important button was the weakest (white on white between black and red) | Each option gives the button its own material or weight |

No per-run marks anywhere on the button: it is identical at 28 and at 120 runs (only the count changes).

## The bar (all options)

Bar 64 high, radius 32, glass, left/right 10, bottom 12, padding 0 8, gap 8. "תפריט" (48, `ink`) and record as today. The runs button fills the middle: 193 x 48 at 390 wide in Hebrew, radius 24. Word 17 / 700 `ink`; count 13.5 / 700 `ink2`, tabular. Screen reader: "מסלולים, 28" (existing). Long press: the name pill (B10), as today.

**Narrow** (recording with the clock, large text): the count drops first; the word and the chevron stay; word 16.

## A · the window (recommended), `RN11`-`RN13`

- Surface: `linear-gradient(180deg,#FFFFFF,#EEF4FA)`; night `#1C2B47` to `#121C30`. Shadow `0 1 2 rgba(10,25,45,.12), 0 5 12 rgba(10,25,45,.12)`. Rim (inside): 1 px `rgba(14,34,51,.10)`, a 1.5 top highlight `rgba(255,255,255,.9)`, a soft bottom inner shadow. No border.
- **The picture:** at the end edge, 132 x 48, the app's own hero picture for the current light, cropped on the peak: centre (128, 172) of the 390 x 844 view at scale 0.5 (Bidara and the saddle); left-to-right: centre (262, 176). It fades into the surface toward the word with a mask (opaque to 46%, transparent at 96%). Never mirrored. Night picture: brightness 1.7.
- **The light of the hour:** the same seven lights as the mountain (dawn, morning, noon, afternoon, sunset, blue hour, night); cross-fade 600 ms when the mountain's light changes.
- Padding start 18; chevron 18 / 2.4 `ink` with a 2 px `halo` glow, 12 from the end, over the picture.
- Narrow: picture 84 wide. Press: scale .965, the picture scales 1.1 around the peak and the window widens to 150 (90 ms).
- **Asset:** in picture mode the bitmap is already in memory (draw the region). In live mode: seven thumbs of 264 x 96 cut from the existing hero pictures by the build (about 6 KB each). Goes into the packaged set; if the 1.2 MB ceiling is at risk, ask the architect. A resort without a hero picture uses C.

## B · the contours, `RN21`-`RN23`

- `tile` surface, border 1 `line`. At the end, 170 x 48: the real contour lines around the top of Kudebi from `site/data/terrain.json` (21 m per px, every 50 m, every 250 m heavier), stroke `ink` .8 at 24% and 1.1 at 42%, masked out toward the word (opaque to 34%, gone at 70%). The summit sits 24 from the end, vertically centred, and the chevron (18 / 2.6, `acc`) sits exactly on it.
- Press: lines turn `acc` at 60%. Opening: the lines scale 2.1 around the summit and fade.
- Asset: one vector per resort, generated by the build from the elevation model (the function `contours()` in the tool is the reference).

## C · the numeral, `RN31`-`RN33`

- Quiet fill `rgba(14,34,51,.06)` (night `rgba(234,241,248,.09)`), no border. Padding 16 start, 12 end, gap 9.
- From the start: a pole 5 x 26, radius 2.5, four equal bands top to bottom black, red, blue, green (a band is omitted if the resort has no run of that colour); the count 29 / 300, tabular (the big light number of the shelf cards); the word 17 / 700; at the end the chevron 18 `ink2`.
- Narrow: number 25, padding 12 / 6. Press: fill `line`.

## D · the handle, `RN41`-`RN43`

- No inner button. The middle zone of the bar (193 x 64) is the target: a grabber 38 x 5, radius 3, `ink` at 26%, and 7 under it the word with the count, centred.
- **Tap** opens as the others. **Pull up:** the bar's own glass follows the finger 1:1 and becomes the shelf's strip panel; "תפריט" and record fade over the first 30% of the travel; the mountain and the cards follow. Release past 35% of the travel or faster than 600 dp/s: settles open (300 ms, decelerate); otherwise springs back (220 ms). One light haptic tick when the threshold is crossed.
- Press: grabber 46 wide at 55%, the zone lifts 3.
- Risk to check on a phone: the pull starts 12 to 76 above the bottom edge, close to Android's home gesture; the zone must sit above the system gesture inset (`systemGestures`), and may need `systemGestureExclusionRects`.

## E · the primary, `RN51`-`RN53`

- The runs button is the one filled button: `ink` with `bg` text, shadow `0 1 2 rgba(6,16,30,.3), 0 6 16 rgba(6,16,30,.28)`, top highlight `rgba(255,255,255,.16)`. Padding 20 start, 13 end. Count at 70%. At the end a 28 disc in `acc` with the chevron (16 / 2.8, `onacc`).
- **"תפריט" turns quiet:** fill `rgba(14,34,51,.06)`, `ink` text. This changes the look approved in the UX pass, so it needs Pini's yes.
- Opening: the dark surface grows and cross-fades to glass.

## The opening (all options; `RNk3` loops it at real speed)

| Step | When (ms from release) | Easing |
|---|---|---|
| Press: button to scale .965 | on touch, 90 | ease-out; light haptic tick on release |
| "תפריט", record, the bar's glass and the button column: fade and sink 10 | 0 to 140 | ease-in |
| **The button's own surface travels and grows into the shelf's strip panel** (from its rect to left/right 12, bottom 246, height 88, radius 22); its content fades by 60% of the way while glass fades in | 0 to 420 | `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)` |
| The mountain rises 120 (picture mode: scales to 1.142 from its top) | 0 to 420 | same |
| The strip's first row and ticks fade in; ticks grow from the baseline | 270 to 470 | same |
| Chips rise 14 and fade in | 140 to 470 | same |
| Cards rise 46 and fade in, centre first, 45 apart | 160 to 550 | same |

- **Close (280 ms):** cards and chips drop and fade (160, `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)`), the panel shrinks back into the button and the mountain returns (`cubic-bezier(.3,0,.2,1)`), the bar's buttons fade in last (120). No stagger.
- **Per option, inside the travelling surface:** A the picture scales 2.1 around the peak and fades (it flies into the mountain); B the contours scale 2.1 around the summit; C the pole scales and fades as the ticks grow; D the whole bar is the surface and follows the finger (drag 0 to about 600 ms, then settle); E dark to glass.
- **Reduced motion:** the shelf cross-fades in 150 ms, the mountain cuts to its raised position, nothing travels, no stagger. D: tap only opens; the pull still works but snaps.

## Recommendation, honestly

**A.** It is the direction Pini was closest to, and the cheapness came from the drawing, not from the idea: here it is the mountain itself in the light on the screen, so the bar changes through the day and belongs only to this app. Its cost: the most detail of the five, and a small per-light asset in live mode. Second choice **C**: the cleanest, works at any resort and language, least experience. B depends on the user reading contour lines; D is the calmest but looks least like a button and sits near the system gesture; E is clear but changes "תפריט" and has nothing of the mountain. A sixth idea (groomed snow with a carved track) was drawn and dropped: at 48 high it read as decoration.

## Honestly

Drawn in the design tool at 390 x 844, checked in a local render, not on a phone. The picture is the canvas's hero picture; the contours are computed from the elevation model; 120 runs is an example. The loops approximate the choreography in CSS (the shelf's ticks do not re-sort in them). No contract or data change; the thumbs of A are the only new packaged asset.
