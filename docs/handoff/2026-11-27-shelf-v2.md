# Shelf v2 (P.4, P.5, P.6), builder report, 27.11.2026

Branch `claude/android-shelf-v2` (not merged). Spec: `design/handoff/2026-11-27-redesign-shelf-layers/README.md`, "Version 2".

## Built
- `ShelfRules.kt`: six sorts (`LIFT, EASY, LONG, SUN, OPEN, SNOW`), `byOpen`, `bySnow`, `lit` (what the mountain lights), `ranks`, tick heights, `ShelfAnswers.ticks/answer` (the big-number table). Tests: `ShelfV2Test`.
- `ShelfStrip.kt`: the strip (ticks 8 wide or narrower, 3 gap / 8 at a group, 12 to 30 high, ring and pointer on the middle run, tap and drag scrub with a haptic tick, 300 ms move staggered 8 ms), the six chips with icons (lit chip takes the colour of what it paints).
- `LiveShelf.kt`: the panel (name, group, position, ticks), chips row (scrolls to keep the lit chip in view), cards 232x172 with the shape panel, big number that rolls 200 ms, caption in `acc`, foot line; source line under the cards for sun and snow; `RankDisc` 1 to 6 on the mountain for "הארוכים".
- Mountain: by lift lights the group's runs and lifts, by colour all runs of the centre card's colour, longest the six; sun, open, snow paint with the existing lens code (`view.setLens`). Layers button removed from `MapColumn`, the chip row and the lens sheet are no longer used (code stays in `LiveLens.kt`, unused).
- P.6: `PeakGeo.raised` scales the picture about its top centre (about 1.14 for 120 dp) so its bottom edge never rises into the screen.
- The runs button flight lands on the strip panel (`onPanel`).
- Empty answers: no sun and no lift report make the chip 40% and a tap shows the line in the strip's first row for 2 s; new snow with no data stays selectable, paints nothing, first row "אין שלג חדש...", ordered by lift.
- Strings: content added 17 `app.shelf_*` keys; I added `app.shelf_unit_cm` (content to confirm).
