## Notes and caveats

1. **DSM vs DTM.** Copernicus GLO-30, SRTM and AW3D30 are surface models (forest canopy, buildings raise the surface). National LiDAR DGM/DTM are bare earth, better for slope angles on pistes.
2. **Attribution stacking.** An app combining sources must show every required credit (OSM/ODbL, Copernicus wording + liability sentence, ©swisstopo, IGN Spain product string, CC BY producers). Spain's licence is the strictest about placement (next to the map).
3. **ODbL share-alike** applies if you distribute a modified piste/lift **database** (e.g. corrected OSM lines shipped as data in the app). A rendered map is a produced work (credit only).
4. **Conflicts to re-check:** Trentino LiDAR licence version (CC BY 4.0 vs CC BY 2.5); Tirol direct-download licence name; AW3D30 credit wording; GLO-90 exact licence text.
5. Several INSPIRE geoportal record URLs now redirect to data.europa.eu; their contents above come from search-result snippets and are marked as such.
