**Bottom line:** credible order of magnitude is 40,000-120,000 Israeli ski trips per season; the 120k figure is a single unattributed press claim. No per-destination split of Israeli skiers found: **unverified**.

### Destinations
- Bulgaria (Bansko, Borovets, Pamporovo) and Georgia (Gudauri) appear in every recent Israeli article as the budget/top destinations; Italy (Cervinia, Passo Tonale, Sella Ronda), France (Tignes, Serre Chevalier, Montgenevre), Austria (Mayrhofen), Andorra as the premium ones. Sources above, plus Israel Hayom: https://www.israelhayom.co.il/travel/tourism-news/article/19073421 (snippet: Gudauri and Bansko recommended for all levels; Borovets favoured by families).
- Skideal ran Hebrew-language kids' ski camps in Les Arcs, Bansko, Passo Tonale and Gudauri (snippet; from search on takebrake/Israeli sites): unverified on the Skideal site itself.
- Package prices quoted for Gudauri: 4 nights in January from EUR 815, 7 nights from EUR 965 (Israel Hayom, snippet); Gudauri midweek from EUR 899 (snippet, https://www.ophirtours.co.il/ski/skideals.html area results).
- Arkia sold Bulgaria ski packages (Bansko, Pamporovo) from USD 699: https://www.maariv.co.il/lifestyle/travel/article-1125069 (snippet).

### Charter season / flights
- No published charter calendar with dates found: **unverified**. Peak demand periods repeatedly named: Hanukkah (Dec), January, Purim (Feb/Mar), Passover (Mako 2024, Globes 2015).
- Tbilisi is served by daily direct flights (Israir, Arkia, Sundor, Georgian Airways) per secretflights (snippet): https://secretflights.co.il/news/ski-holiday-tips
- Arkia winter 2025/26 schedule includes Sofia and Tbilisi, no per-route dates published in that article: https://teutza.com/en/blog_travel/arkias-winter-2025-2026-flight-schedule-25-global-destinations/

## 3. Georgian resorts visitor numbers

- **2023 (whole year, all quarters, not ski-only):** 447,696 international visitors to Georgian mountain resorts, 71% of them to Gudauri (~318k). Domestic: 324,326 visitors, 71% to Bakuriani. International visits were 83% of 2019 level. Source: PMCG Quarterly Tourism Update 2023 (based on GNTA visitor survey), https://pmcg-i.com/app/uploads/2024/04/MTU-2023-ENG.pdf (read directly). Note: "MTA data could not be obtained."
- Gudauri skier visits 55,000 (2010) to 170,000 (2015): Ecosign master-plan page, https://www.ecosign.com/projects/gudauri (snippet).
- Gudauri >276,000 visitors in 2016/17, +37.1% YoY: Galt & Taggart via Statista, https://www.statista.com/statistics/824172/number-of-visitors-bakuriani-gudauri-winter-resorts-georgia/ (numbers paywalled; figure from snippet).
- Season 2025/26: Gudauri added 6 km of pistes to 70 km and two new 6-seat lifts (2,400 p/h each): https://mta.ski/en/presscenter/GudauriOpening2025 (snippet).
- Bakuriani, Tetnuldi/Hatsvali (Svaneti), Goderdzi per-season numbers: **unverified** (not found from GNTA/MTA/MRDC).
- Note: MRDC was the earlier name; resorts are now run by Mountain Trails Agency (mta.ski).
